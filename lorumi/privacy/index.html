Privacy Policy

Lorumi
Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Information Handled by Lorumi

Lorumi handles the practice information you create in the app, including written answers, saved review state, voice recordings, raw, edited, and confirmed transcripts, and generated practice feedback. Lorumi does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or payment card details.

2. Microphone and Voice Recordings

Lorumi requests microphone access only when you choose to record a speaking or interview-practice answer. The resulting audio file is stored locally on your device so you can play it back, transcribe it, receive practice feedback, or delete it. Lorumi does not continuously listen to or record audio in the background.

3. Speech Recognition

When you request transcription, Lorumi uses Apple's Speech framework and an installed Korean speech-recognition asset to process the selected recording on your device. Lorumi stores the resulting transcript locally so you can review, edit, confirm, and delete it. Lorumi does not upload your recording or transcript to a developer-operated transcription server.

4. AI Practice Feedback

When available and requested, Lorumi uses Apple's Foundation Models framework and the on-device Apple Intelligence language model to evaluate a written answer or confirmed transcript against the practice question. Generated feedback is stored locally on your device. Lorumi does not send your answer, transcript, prompt, or generated feedback to a developer-operated AI server. AI feedback is reference-only practice assistance and is not an official assessment, score, eligibility decision, or guarantee of any result.

5. How Information Is Used

Your practice information is used only to provide Lorumi's features, including saving and reviewing answers, recording and playing back speech, producing and editing transcripts, generating practice feedback, and showing saved review progress.

6. Data Storage and Security

Lorumi stores practice data locally in the app's container using Apple's local storage technologies. Stored practice data and recording files use iOS file protection and are excluded from device backups. Lorumi does not operate its own server for your recordings, answers, transcripts, or generated feedback.

7. Third-Party Services

Lorumi uses Apple platform services needed for app functionality, including the microphone and audio frameworks, the Speech framework, Apple Intelligence, the Foundation Models framework, local storage, and the App Store. Lorumi does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not use third-party AI or transcription services, and does not sell your personal data. Apple's handling of information through its services is governed by Apple's policies.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your Lorumi recordings, answers, transcripts, or generated feedback with third parties. The current version processes these materials on your device and does not transmit them to Woorlds.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Practice data remains on your device until you delete it. Lorumi provides controls to delete recordings, transcripts, and feedback individually and a setting to delete all practice data. Deleting Lorumi from your device also removes data stored in its local app container.

10. Children's Privacy

Lorumi does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account, analytics service, advertising service, or developer-operated server. Practice content entered or recorded in the app remains subject to the local processing and storage practices described in this policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Lorumi's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact woorlds@gmail.com.
