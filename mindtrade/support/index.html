Advertising Privacy

MindTrade uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Where required, Google presents advertising privacy choices. If an advertising privacy-options row is available in MindTrade settings, use it to review or change those choices.

Data and Backup

MindTrade stores your trading journal data on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, your backup files are stored in your personal iCloud account. You can delete local app data by deleting entries in the app or deleting the app from your device.
