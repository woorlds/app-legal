Privacy Policy

Signalbook
Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Information Handled by Signalbook

Signalbook is a free investment reasoning and reflection journal for iPhone. It handles the records you enter, including titles, dates, categories, notes, reasoning steps, linked stock names and codes, expected and observed directions, source links, review dates, reflections, and drafts. It also stores app preferences. Signalbook does not require an app account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone access, or payment card details.

2. How Your Journal Data Is Used

Your journal data is used to create, edit, search, organize, and review records, restore drafts, and provide backup and restoration when you choose those features. Signalbook does not transmit journal content or drafts to Woorlds or Google AdMob, and does not include that content in advertising requests or use it to personalize ads. Woorlds does not operate a server for your journal data.

3. Local Storage and Optional iCloud Backup

Journal records are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData technology. Drafts and preferences are also stored on your device. Signalbook does not automatically synchronize journal records between devices. When you choose to create an iCloud backup, the app writes a file containing saved journal records and backup metadata to its iCloud container under your Apple account. You can list, restore, and delete these backup files in the app. This optional file backup is separate from live synchronization and from Apple's device backup services.

Apple processes iCloud data according to your account settings and its policies. Device backups may also include local app data according to your Apple backup settings. Local data is subject to iOS security protections and your device settings. Signalbook may retain local safety copies during backup restoration and recovery.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Signalbook uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices where required. It uses Apple platform services for local storage, optional iCloud backup, and App Store distribution. Signalbook does not integrate separate third-party analytics or advertising mediation services.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device identifiers (including advertising identifiers when permitted), information about ads shown and ad interactions, app interactions, crash logs, and performance or diagnostic data. Google may use this information for ad delivery, advertising measurement and analytics, service improvement, and fraud prevention. Signalbook does not request precise location for advertising or pass journal content or a custom user identifier to Google.

5. Advertising Privacy Choices

Depending on your region, Google UMP may show a form for advertising privacy choices. When Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, you can reopen the form from Signalbook's settings. Non-personalized or limited ads may still use technical data for delivery, security, and reporting. Signalbook does not present an App Tracking Transparency permission request or request notification permission.

See the Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Apple's services are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and Support Requests

We do not sell your journal data or share it with advertising providers. If you choose iCloud backup, your saved journal content is transferred to Apple's iCloud service as described above. Google may process advertising-related data as described in this policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you send. We use these messages to respond and resolve issues. Avoid including sensitive journal content in support messages unless necessary.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local journal data and drafts remain on your device until removed. Deleting a journal record does not remove copies already saved in backup files or local restoration safety copies. Deleting the app removes its local app container; offloading the app can retain its data. Deleting the app does not automatically delete iCloud backup files. You can delete the app's iCloud backups using its backup management screen; Apple backup and storage controls govern other retained copies. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us below. Google manages advertising data retention according to its policies and applicable privacy choices.

8. Children's Privacy

Signalbook does not provide accounts or knowingly collect children's journal content through a developer-operated server. Advertising services may process the information described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Signalbook's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published here with a revised update date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Woorlds at woorlds@gmail.com.
