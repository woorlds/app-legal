App Support

Thank you for using Signalbook, an investment reasoning and reflection journal for iPhone.

Contact Us

For bug reports, feature requests, privacy-related questions, or other support requests, email woorlds@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Signalbook version shown in the app's settings, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots can help, but please remove private journal content or other sensitive information first.

Journal Data and iCloud Backup

Signalbook stores your journal records, drafts, and preferences on your iPhone. It does not require an app account or automatically synchronize journal records between devices. In Settings > Backup & restore, you can create an optional iCloud backup and list, restore, or delete saved backup files. These files are stored under your Apple account.

If iCloud backup is unavailable, check that you are signed in to your Apple account and that iCloud Drive is enabled. When reporting a backup issue, tell us whether you were creating, listing, restoring, or deleting a backup and include any message shown in the app.

Deleting the app removes its local data, so avoid deleting it as a first troubleshooting step if you want to keep your records. Deleting a record does not remove copies already saved in backup files, and deleting the app does not automatically delete its iCloud backups. You can manage those files from the app's backup screen.

Advertising Privacy

Signalbook uses Google AdMob to display banner and native advertisements. Where required, Google presents advertising privacy choices. If an advertising privacy-options row is available in Signalbook settings, use it to review or change those choices. Signalbook does not send your journal content to Google AdMob or use that content to personalize ads.
